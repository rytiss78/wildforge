# Illustrated art

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then atlas-packed and reduced for the game. No reference screenshots are shipped. The screenshots supplied by the user guided the art direction: outlined illustrated objects, small material textures and warm opaque desktop UI.

`icons.png`: 768×512, 24 cells, 128×128 each, indexed 192-color PNG. It supplies weapons and power families; generated combinations also show each constituent power. `grass.png`, `stone.png`, `bark.png`, `paper.png`: 256×256, indexed 96-color PNGs. These original five images are opaque. Their source PNG size is 378,262 bytes. Godot also creates optimized imported textures; these sizes describe source PNGs, not GPU memory.

## Final generation prompts

Icon atlas: “Create a production game UI sprite atlas, one rectangular image with an EXACT 6 column by 4 row grid of 24 illustrated item icons, evenly spaced identical square cells, no grid lines, no text, no letters. Solid uniform pale ivory background #f6eddc, NO transparency. Each icon centered and wholly inside its cell with generous 15% empty padding. Style: charming hand-drawn cozy adventure game inventory illustrations, thin slightly irregular brown ink contours, pastel flat colors with two simple shadow tones, small material details, three-quarter view, readable at 64px, NOT glossy 3D renders, NOT pixel art. First row left to right: quirky mint single-barrel pistol; orange wooden triple-barrel shotgun; red flame sprayer with fire canister; green poison sprayer with corked vial; blue icy ray gun with ice crystal; long purple rail rifle. Second row: yellow lightning coil weapon; toothed steel circular saw with wooden grip; coral rocket launcher with pointed rocket; lavender friendly ghost spirit; planted pink flower in terracotta pot; tiny squat mechanical tripod gun turret. Third row: red fire charm; green poison potion cork bottle; blue ice crystal; pink heart with two vampire fangs; blue wooden shield with brass rim; golden stack of coins. Fourth row: brass key; green boots; horseshoe magnet; thorny leafy branch; blindfold goggles; open spell book with purple star. Exactly these 24 cells, no additional decorations outside objects. Consistent scale and stroke weight throughout. Original game asset inspired by cozy hand-illustrated objects, warm lively colors, minimal file-size-friendly flat shading.”

Material atlas: “Game environment texture atlas, exactly 2x2 square quadrants occupying full image, four simple seamless material swatches, no text, no borders, no objects, no perspective. Top left: soft sage green meadow ground with small hand drawn darker tufts and tiny cream clover flecks, very sparse, no large features. Top right: warm grey sandstone with fine irregular brown ink cracks, pale pebble-like speckles. Bottom left: terracotta brown wood bark, thin wavy vertical brown hand drawn grain and subtle warm ochre highlights. Bottom right: ivory cream paper with very faint warm fiber speckles, mostly clean. Style cozy illustrated adventure, muted pastel colors, thin brown pen strokes, understated tiny details, flat painted shading, not photographic, not glossy, legible small repeating 256px world textures. Each quadrant separately seamless edge to edge. Original small texture atlas to apply to native 3D cartoon terrain and props.”


Achievement badges reuse the approved item illustrations as challenge compositions. `scripts/create-achievement-icons.py` combines the atlas art with code-drawn crown, exploration, combination and stitched badge motifs. `achievement-art.json` maps stable achievement IDs to tiles. The native game loads two 960px atlases; The standalone achievement catalog includes 100 paired 256px PNGs. No external art was copied.

World meshes use the same warm contour colour and pastel palette. Character rigs, weapon sockets, prop shapes and six biome decorations are authored procedurally. Character and chunk mesh batching preserve vertex colours while reducing draw calls. Tiny paper/grass/bark/stone textures provide painted detail. All six biome voice lines are original text rendered offline with the same licensed Kokoro voice; provenance is in `../voices/voice-provenance.json`.

The 0.5 hero badges used seven small game-rendered portraits; 0.6 replaces those with active painted hero illustrations. Near-camera foliage uses an opaque cutout in both paint and contour passes, so leaves cannot conceal the hero behind a thin outline shell.

## Build 0.6 painted actors and worlds

The active world now uses 21 distinct 256px transparent hero illustrations, 36 exotic 256px creature illustrations, 24 256px transparent scenery props and six 256px ground textures. They were generated with the built-in imagegen tool and imported through scripts/prepare-illustrations.py. Weapons directly reuse the approved 128px weapon icon cells as alpha cutout world props. Art and collision are separate: full 3D terrain/movement, camera-facing illustrated characters and scenery, animated weapon sockets, recoil, hit jolts and squash/bob movement. The earlier mesh-sculpted hero portraits have been removed.

Exact generation and cutout prompts are saved in art-0.6-prompts.json. Original icon/material prompts above remain relevant to the UI, old small trim materials and weapon source atlas. The newer sprites preserve alpha. Total current PNG source art is about 10.7 MiB; Godot creates separate optimized GPU imports. Achievement portraits now composite the active transparent hero illustration over the paper badge while preserving all achievement IDs.
