Wildforge 0.8.2 brings the completed content and exploration update to Windows.

- Smaller 800 m map with 24 paid chests per realm and five reward choices.
- 400 added items, skills and weapon variants; 759 total catalog entries.
- Richer painted icons with distinct card seals and individually illustrated new weapons.
- Jump Boots increase jump height; Extra Hop adds an air jump.
- Clear beaches and an animated deadly sea around the island.
- Fully revealed minimap with merchants, supply signals, chests, landmarks, pots, potions, gates and party markers.
- Co-op merchant transaction timing fixed to avoid a stuck pending purchase.
- Merchants, banish, elemental reactions, co-op rescue/pings, supply defence, 108 achievements and hero quips.
- Fragile ordinary ranged enemies with longer attack cooldowns; bosses and playable heroes retain separate rules.

Download the Windows ZIP, extract it, and run Wildforge.exe. Keep Wildforge.pck beside the executable. SHA256SUMS.txt provides the archive checksum. Existing career data is retained.

Validation includes integrated gameplay/render checks and a packaged local co-op host/guest run. See the repository's build notes for evidence and test limits.
