Moss prompt: Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: seamless repeating square albedo ground texture for a third-person whimsical storybook action game called Wildforge. Create ONE flat top-down tile of a mossy forest floor: subtle broad muted sage and olive painted patches, soft brushed grassy tufts, tiny sparse ochre fallen leaves, a few shallow earth and pebble details. Premium hand-painted videogame material, calm low contrast, no lighting direction or cast shadows, no perspective, no objects standing up, no flowers, no characters, no text or border. Texture fills entire square, continuous tileable edges; details evenly scattered without a central focal point, mostly quiet organic color variation. Avoid busy wallpaper patterns, repeating decorative symbols, bright highlights and photorealism. Intended to read gently from a 10-meter-high gameplay camera.

Stone prompt: Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one seamless square top-down albedo tile for a polished storybook fantasy videogame. Create quiet hand-painted weathered stone ground: broad cool slate-grey and warm grey patches, softly eroded flat rock plates, very subtle tiny chips and hairline cracks, sparse dusty pebbles. Low contrast painterly gouache, no perspective or directional shadows, no lighting gradients, tileable continuous edges, no central focal point. Mostly calm broad surfaces, not busy wallpaper, no symbols, no stars, no flowers, no crystals, no text, no border. Will be tintable for moon craters, cloud ruins and volcanic biomes.

Shader uses restrained luminance variation over biome-specific colors. Original six illustrated materials remain in repository for older art references/sky; new ground materials are versioned and do not overwrite them.
