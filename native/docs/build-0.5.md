# Wildforge 0.5 — illustrated exploration update

Implemented the approved build plan in the native Godot game. `build/Wildforge.exe` is the single playable Windows build; keep its sibling PCK and DLLs.

- Seven deliberately shaped illustrated heroes share an animated character rig. First and second weapons occupy the hands; the third grows an extra arm. Equipment follows upgrades and replacements. The menu renders the same character and starter weapon.
- Seventeen held weapon models have grips, barrels, coils/canisters, recoil, pulses or saw movement. Turrets use a held deployment rig and share the three-weapon limit. Weapon sounds use cached synthesis, pooled positional channels and controlled continuous loops. Florist flowers grow, sway and bloom along the player's walking trail.
- Six biomes appear in each approximately 1 km wide chapter: Clover Woods, Puffcap Marsh, Honey Dunes, Frost Peaks, Cinder Hills and Prism Gardens. Terrain/collisions/scenery stream by chunk. Hills, paths and four raised ruins support exploration. Biome names appear in the HUD and receive one natural neural announcement per run. The existing timed bosses and chapter gates remain.
- Each biome has its own creature and scenery set. Higher-threat regions increase health, damage and rewards. Toad poison, charging crabs, chilling penguins, burning lizards and blinding beetles add regional combat differences. Ordinary enemies have a minimum 1.65 m model and solid capsules, with spatial separation and bounded spawn searches. Grass remains short.
- F/Xbox A jumps and double-jumps; Ctrl/Xbox B slams from the air. Large drops cause one landing damage event. Six movement bonus types add height, air jumps, fall protection, slam strength/radius and bouncing. Solid outer boundaries, coordinate bounds, streamed floor coverage and last-safe recovery prevent leaving the map.
- Level-up has an audible flourish, character particles, vibration and an opaque nameplate before the paused choice screen. Burn flames, poison colour/droplets, ice crystals and blindness indicators follow status lifetime. Actors have locomotion, attack, hit and finale motions; scenery has a subtle breeze and chest lids open.
- Elites drop four consumables: Poison Guard, Fire Guard, Speed Juice and XP Juice. E/X activates the highlighted bottle. Buffs last 25 seconds, pause with gameplay, carry through gates and refresh without stacking duration. Temporary effects stay separate from permanent cards. HUD icons show names/countdowns. Uncollected drops expire after two minutes of gameplay.
- The 272 item/skill catalog entries each award one beneficial bonus. Common baselines are normalized across families; rarity strengthens that bonus. Cards show a numeric benefit and the Common comparison. Discrete jumps/lives/shots use whole values; capped offers remain useful.
- All 100 stable achievement IDs have illustrated locked/unlocked art. Hero badges use rendered game models; combination/build/challenge badges reuse the approved illustrations. Steam definitions reference the 200 supplied 256 px images. Existing career data is preserved.

Validation: eight Node tests; native gameplay/save/UI smoke checks, including actual landing damage and ground-slam impacts; rendered exported Windows smoke run; 36-second crowded-fight benchmark across six biomes with roughly 100 enemies. Detailed reports are alongside this document. The exported stress run measured a 19.29 ms 95th-percentile frame time across 3,580 sampled frames; release memory monitoring was unavailable. Screenshots were reviewed at 1920×1080. Tests use a separate career profile.

The performance benchmark uses Ryzen 7 5800X / Radeon RX 9060 XT. It stresses crowded fights and repeated chunk transitions; results are a development measurement, not a minimum hardware specification or a guarantee for every encounter.

Physical Xbox buttons/rumble, live Steam achievement synchronization and longer human play sessions remain release qualification work. No Steam publication was performed. This is the updated development alpha, not the first stable Steam release.
