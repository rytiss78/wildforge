# Twenty more funny ideas — choose before implementation

The chest sneeze and third-hand thumbs-up are already approved for 0.7.9. These twenty additional ideas are proposals only.

1. **Squeaky Boots:** a rare movement item gives footsteps tiny rubber squeaks.
2. **Party Hat Bosses:** huge bosses wear tiny party hats that wobble during attacks.
3. **Duck Parade:** a harmless family of ducks occasionally crosses a safe path.
4. **Snack Hand:** the third hand eats a biscuit during a short idle animation.
5. **Drama Queen Mimic:** a fake box sees your weapons, panics and runs away with bonus loot.
6. **Turbo Snail Race:** oversized snails hold a ridiculously slow race beside the battlefield.
7. **Bread Toasting:** Sir Loaf gets visibly toasted edges when protected from fire.
8. **Monster Slip:** a banana-themed card makes enemies slip with a brief squash animation.
9. **Disco Ice:** frozen enemies occasionally become colourful disco sculptures.
10. **Tiny Crown, Huge King:** an enormous boss carries a crown barely bigger than a coin.
11. **Flower Headbangers:** powerful Florist blooms nod along to the metal music.
12. **Potion Burp:** drinking a potion gives the hero a tiny matching coloured bubble.
13. **Goblin Receipts:** economy bonuses produce a comically long paper receipt when a box opens.
14. **Moon Cheese:** some moon rocks have cheese holes and an occasional nibble missing.
15. **Ice-Cream Truck:** a cheerful roaming vendor offers optional temporary treats.
16. **Cactus Hug:** Prickle Rick offers an idle hug, then remembers the spikes.
17. **Laundry Ghosts:** some ghosts wear socks, towels or old pillowcases.
18. **Bubble Bathtub:** Admiral Bubbles leaves a short harmless trail of soap bubbles after a dash.
19. **Victory Complaints:** heroes react to winning with short personality-specific gestures.
20. **Emergency Chicken Armour:** a defensive item adds a tiny chicken helmet, keeping the health orb visible.

Choose any numbers. None of these adds a negative item bonus or replaces combat clarity with a joke.
