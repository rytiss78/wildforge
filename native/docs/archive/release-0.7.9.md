Brighter worlds, more satisfying upgrades and a little extra nonsense.

- Discover six colourful landmarks, from a giant bloom to a planet garden.
- Skill cards have detailed illustrations distinct from guns, animated reveals and different selection sounds for each power family.
- Reroll unwanted choices with coins: press R, Xbox Y or the button. Full weapon slots keep offering upgrades to your weapons.
- Boxes sneeze coins. Your third hand celebrates weapon upgrades with a thumbs-up.
- Fixed range upgrades, fast bullets missing enemies, weak thorns damage, frozen enemy attacks and selection focus when switching controls.
- Reduced repeated work in card generation, icons and previews.

The packaged build passed focused feature checks, a crowded six-biome run and co-op checks. Occasional hitches remain; this is still an alpha.

Extract the Windows ZIP and launch **Wildforge.exe**. Existing saves remain compatible.
