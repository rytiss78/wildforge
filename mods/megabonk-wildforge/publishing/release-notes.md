Wildforge meets Megabonk: 21 heroes and perks, 694 cards, 65 weapons, 54 enemy forms and 875 original icons.

Development alpha 0.3.0 adds skills compatibility, mixed rosters and content pools, and HP fill inside Wildforge actor orbs. Native actors retain their original indicators.

Requires Windows x64 Megabonk 1.0.69 and BepInEx 6 IL2CPP x64 build 6.0.738. Loader installed separately. Extract the manual ZIP into the game folder. Saves use a separate Wildforge profile.

All 342 skills passed acquisition, two-stack resolution and removal checks. Weapons and enemy AI use native adapters; full-run balance and complete source behavior parity remain in development.

Source, screenshot, installation and evidence: https://github.com/rytiss78/wildforge/tree/main/mods/megabonk-wildforge

AI tools assisted development and generated significant portions of original illustrations. No game assemblies or personal saves are included.
